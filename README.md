### 📌 Project Overview
**Client Problem:** Analyze investor behavior to understand preferences for investment avenues, savings objectives, duration, and information sources.

**Dataset:** 40 Investors | Avg Age: 27.8 Years | 92.5% Investment Adoption Rate

**Tools Used:** Power BI Desktop, Power Query, DAX, Data Modeling, Excel  


### 📂 Repository Structure
### 🚀 7-Phase Project Deliverables (Part of All Assignments)

**Phase 1: Data Exploration & KPI Summary**
- Found Mutual Funds (18) is most preferred avenue
- Retirement Planning is top savings objective (22)

**Phase 2: Demographic Segmentation (Gender Analysis)**
- Female investors prefer Mutual Funds
- Male investors are more diversified

**Phase 3: Objective-Based Analysis**
- Capital Appreciation (25+) is #1 investment objective
- Retirement Plan is #1 savings objective

**Phase 4: Behavioral Analysis (Duration & Monitoring)**
- Preferred Duration: 3-5 Years (19) & 1-3 Years (18)
- 29 out of 40 investors monitor MONTHLY

**Phase 5: Investment Driver Analysis (Reasons)**
- Equity: Capital Appreciation (30)
- Bonds: Assured Returns (26)
- Mutual Funds: Better Returns (24)
- Fixed Deposits: Risk-Free (19)

**Phase 6: Information Channel Analysis**
- Financial Consultants are most trusted source (40%)
- Followed by Newspaper & Magazines

**Phase 7: Executive Master Dashboard (Final Deliverable)**
- Combined all 6 phases into one interactive dashboard
- Added slicers for Gender, Duration & Avenue
- Key Business Insights documented on dashboard

### 💡 Key Business Insights
- 92.5% of investors use investment avenues
- Mutual Funds dominate among young investors
- Retirement planning is primary goal for this age group
- Monthly monitoring is preferred behavior
- Financial consultants have highest influence
